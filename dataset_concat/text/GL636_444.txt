TARGET simplified Chinese: 芜湖; traditional Chinese: 蕪湖; pinyin: Wúhú; literally "Weedy Lake") is a prefecture-level city in southeastern RED province, China. Sitting on the southeast bank of the GREEN, Wuhu borders BLUE to the southeast, YELLOW and MAGENTA to the southwest, CYAN city to the northwest, ORANGE city to the northeast, PURPLE Province to the east, and is approximately 90 km (56 mi) southwest of PINK. As of 2017, the city had a population of approximately 3,696,000 officially registered inhabitants.[1]
