TARGET is a regency (GREENn: kabupaten) in the northwestern part of RED province in GREEN. It covers an area of 1,902.37 km2, and at the 2010 Census it had a population of 1,733,869:[1] the latest official estimate (as at January 2014) is 1,755,136. Its capital is the town of BLUE in the northeast corner of the regency.
