TARGET is a district in southwest RED, lying along the country's border with GREEN and BLUE. The administrative center is YELLOW. The district of Kgalagadi covers a large part of the MAGENTA. It has a total area of 105,200 km² and has a population of 42,000 (2001). More than one-third of the district is covered by the CYAN, which extends into BLUE, and which is a major tourist attraction.
