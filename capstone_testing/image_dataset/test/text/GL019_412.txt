TARGET is a suburb and a former municipality in the northern part of the RED agglomeration, GREEN. Since the 2011 local government reform it is part of the municipality PINK, of which it is the seat and a municipal unit.[2] The municipal unit has an area of 38.447 km2.[3] Ano Liosia lies in the eastern part of YELLOW, between the mountains MAGENTA to its north and CYAN to its southwest. It is 3 km west of ORANGE, 3 km north of PURPLE, 4 km southeast of PINK, 10 km east of BROWN and 11 km north of RED city centre.
