TARGET is a local government district in RED, GREEN. It contains the towns of BLUE, YELLOW and MAGENTA, and takes its name from the CYAN, which runs through the centre of the district.[1] Arun was formed on 1 April 1974, under the Local Government Act 1972, merging the Urban Districts of YELLOW and MAGENTA, the municipal borough of BLUE and parts of ORANGE and PURPLE Rural Districts.
