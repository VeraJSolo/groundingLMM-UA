Located in south-central RED, Iburi stretches 152 kilometres (94 mi) East-West and 98 kilometres (61 mi) North-South. Iburi covers an area of 9,698 square kilometres (3,744 sq mi). Iburi borders GREEN to the West, BLUE, YELLOW, and MAGENTA to the North, and CYAN to the East. On its South side, Iburi has 218 kilometres (135 mi) of coastline with the ORANGE.[1]
