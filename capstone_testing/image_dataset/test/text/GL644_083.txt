TARGET simplified Chinese: 元氏县; traditional Chinese: 元氏縣; pinyin: Yuánshì Xiàn) is located in the southwest of RED, North GREEN, 40 kilometres (25 mi) to the south of BLUE, the provincial capital. Yuanshi County borders YELLOW to the south, MAGENTA and CYAN to the east, ORANGE to the west and PURPLE to its north.
