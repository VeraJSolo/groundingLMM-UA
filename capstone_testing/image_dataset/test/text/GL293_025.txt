TARGET is an administrative[1] and municipal[5] district (raion), one of the twenty-five in RED, GREEN. It is located in the west of the oblast and borders with BLUE in the north, YELLOW in the east, MAGENTA in the south, CYAN of ORANGE of PURPLE in the southwest, and with PINK of BROWN, also of PURPLE, in the west. The area of the district is 1,507.67 square kilometers (582.11 sq mi).[1] Its administrative center is the urban locality (a settlement) of Krasny.[1] Population: 12,895 (2010 Census);[2] 15,537 (2002 Census);[7] 18,758 (1989 Census).[8] The population of Krasny accounts for 33.7% of the district's total population.[2]
