TARGET spelled as Jawzjan or Jozjan (Persian: ولایت جوزجان‎, Pashto: جوزجان ولايت‎), is one of the thirty-four provinces of RED, located in the north of the country bordering neighboring GREEN. The province is divided into 11 districts and contains hundreds of villages. It has a population of about 512,100,[2] which is multi-ethnic and mostly agriculturalists. BLUE is the capital of Jozjan province.
