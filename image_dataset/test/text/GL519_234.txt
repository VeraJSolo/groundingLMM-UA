TARGET is a district in the RED state of GREEN. This district was carved out from BLUE on 28 September 2011 as Prabudh Nagar and renamed Shamli in July 2012. Shamli is the headquarters of the district. Shamli is located along the YELLOW–MAGENTA (National Highway-709B), CYAN-ORANGE (Nation Highway-709A) and PURPLE-PINK (National Highway-709AD) Highways.
