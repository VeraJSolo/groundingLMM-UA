TARGET is a market place and old mining town in the RED state of GREEN, 68 mi (109 km). N.W. of BLUE by rail. Pop. (2001) 6,400. It is situated in the deep YELLOW Valley, dominated on the east by the MAGENTA 1871m, (6140 ft), on the west by the CYAN 2084 m (6830 ft), and on the south by the ORANGE 1465 m (5030 ft). It has a medieval fortified church, a Gothic edifice founded by PURPLE in the 13th century and rebuilt in the 16th century.[3]
