TARGET is a province in northern RED, bordering GREEN to the east, CYAN to the north, YELLOW and MAGENTA to the west. It covers an area of 6,575 km² and has a population of 129,618 in 2010. The population was 186,953 in 2000. The name Gümüşhane means silver house. The city has a rich mining (silver and bronze) history and was the source of exports for CYAN.
