TARGET ᠬᠣᠰᠢᠭᠤ Далад қосиу Dalad qosiɣu; simplified Chinese: 达拉特旗; traditional Chinese: 達拉特旗; pinyin: Dálātè Qí) is a banner of western RED, People's Republic of China, lying on the southern (right) bank of the GREEN. It is under the administration of BLUE, although it is closer to the city of YELLOW, 33 kilometres (21 mi) to the north-northwest.
