TARGET is a canton in the RED, GREEN. It is located in the south-east of the province, bordered by the cantons of BLUE, YELLOW, MAGENTA, CYAN, and ORANGE. It is the "agricultural, farming, and craftsmanship capital of YELLOW." Gonzanamá covers 1272 km² at an altitude of 2045 m, with a population of 17,276. The principal town is PURPLE.
