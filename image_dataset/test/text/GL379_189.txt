TARGET is a 17 miles (27 km) long paved bicycle and pedestrian path along the RED in Northern Virginia between GREEN and George Washington's home at BLUE. The trail connects the easternmost portions of YELLOW, MAGENTA, and CYAN, and travels mostly on dedicated trail with a small portion on city streets. As part of ORANGE, the MVT opened in April 1972 as a gravel path and was subsequently expanded and paved.[1][2]
