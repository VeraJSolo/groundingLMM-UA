TARGET is an island in RED province, GREEN. It forms part of the chain of the BLUE, with the YELLOW separating it from PINK to the west and the CYAN between it and BROWN to the east. It is roughly circular, with a "tail" to the southwest, about 70 kilometres (43 miles) across and a total area of about 4,514 square kilometres (1,743 square miles). The provincial capital and largest city on the island is PURPLE. Lombok is somewhat similar in size and density, and shares some cultural heritage with the neighboring island of PINK to the west. However, it is administratively part of RED, along with the larger and more sparsely populated island of BROWN to the east. Lombok is surrounded by a number of smaller islands locally called Gili.
