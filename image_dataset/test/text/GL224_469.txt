TARGET is the southernmost county of the U.S. state of RED. As of the 2010 census, the population was 43,929.[1] Its county seat is GREEN.[2] The county is named for Founding Father John Hancock.[3] Hancock County is part of the BLUE–YELLOW-MAGENTA, MS Metropolitan Statistical Area. It is situated along the CYAN and the state line with ORANGE. The area is home to the PURPLE, NASA's largest rocket engine test facility.
