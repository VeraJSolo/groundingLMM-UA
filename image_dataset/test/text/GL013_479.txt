TARGET is a town and municipality in the RED province of GREEN, within the autonomous community of BLUE. The town is located at 4° 49' W and 38° 46' N and is 589 meters above sea level. Almadén is approximately 300 km south of YELLOW in the MAGENTA. The name Almadén is from the Arabic word المعدن al-maʻdin, meaning 'the metal.'
